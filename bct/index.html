Boot Camp Translation (BCT) Facilitator Certification

A proven, community-engaged method that supports partnerships of community members and researchers to translate medical evidence into messages and actions that fit local life.

About the Training

Open enrollment

May 19–21, 2027. Location to be announced. A cohort of up to 20 participants. Cost to be announced.

On-site for your team

We bring the full training to your institution or network and schedule it with you.

Certification offers you

  • A 3-day training that will leave you with:
    • A clear understanding of when and why to use BCT and the specific steps involved
    • Skills to conduct your own BCT
    • A draft plan for a BCT in your own project
  • Follow-up coaching as you develop or conduct your first BCT

Who it is for

Academic researchers and practice-based research network (PBRN) staff. Experience in community-engaged research and group facilitation is strongly recommended.

Your certified BCT trainers

Donald E. Nease, Jr., MD; John (Jack) Westfall, MD, MPH; and team.

Developed with community members in rural Colorado, BCT has been used in over 70 research and quality improvement projects on topics covering cancer prevention, hypertension, asthma, diabetes, mental health, and more. Work has been published across a wide range of peer-reviewed journals.
